Our Founder

The story behind Shivam Opticals

Arvind Dwivedi

Some businesses begin with investment.
Shivam Opticals began with determination.

In 1991 tragedy struck our family when Mr. Shyam Narayan Dwivedi, an entrepreneur and lawyer, lost his life unexpectedly. His passing left an entire family dependent on his eldest son, Arvind Dwivedi, who was still studying in Class 11.

Forced to put aside his own dreams of higher education, he stepped forward to support his family. Over the years, he tried one venture after another — not because success was guaranteed, but because giving up was never an option.

With the encouragement of his father-in-law, Mr. Ram Kumar Pandey, he opened an electronics shop in 1992. Despite his passion for electronics, the business struggled in a small-town economy where many customers couldn't afford such products, and unpaid credit became difficult for a young entrepreneur to recover.

He then explored other ventures, including a local cinema projector business, government supply work, and a tent business. Every setback became another lesson rather than the end of the journey.

In 1995, a government-allotted PCO finally brought financial stability to the family. But even then, he kept looking ahead.

Across the road stood Sitapur Eye Hospital. Seeing people in need of quality eyewear — and noticing an opportunity to offer better service — he introduced a small collection of sunglasses and spectacles alongside the PCO.

That simple decision became the beginning of Shivam Opticals, named after his newborn son, affectionately called Shivam.

What started with just a few frames soon earned the trust of families across Sultanpur through honest advice, fair pricing, and genuine care. As demand grew, prescription eyewear, advanced lenses, and professional eye care became the foundation of the business.

Today, more than 30 years later, Shivam Opticals continues to serve the community with the same values on which it was built:

Hard work. Integrity. Trust.

Frequently Asked

Lens questions, answered

Everything you need to know about bluecut, anti-glare and photochromic lenses before you buy.

What are Bluecut Glasses and do I really need them?
Bluecut glasses contain a special coating that filters out harmful high-energy blue light emitted by phones, laptops, LED screens and tablets. If you spend 4+ hours daily on a screen — for work, study or gaming — bluecut lenses noticeably reduce eye strain, headaches and sleep disruption. They can be added to clear glasses, prescription lenses, or worn as zero-power protective eyewear.
How are Anti Glare lenses different from Bluecut?
Anti glare (AR) lenses use a multi-layer optical coating to eliminate reflections from headlights, screens and overhead lighting. Bluecut specifically blocks blue light wavelengths from digital screens. For most modern users — especially night drivers and screen-heavy professionals — we recommend a combined AR + Bluecut lens for full-day comfort.
What are Photochromic lenses and how do they work?
Photochromic lenses automatically darken when exposed to UV sunlight outdoors and return to clear when you go back indoors. They effectively give you two pairs in one — regular eyeglasses indoors and sunglasses outdoors — without ever switching frames. Perfect for commuters, two-wheeler riders and field workers in Sultanpur's bright sun.
Is the eye testing really free at Shivam Opticals?
Yes — completely free, no appointment needed. Walk into either our Dariyapur or Kasba branch and our trained optometrists will run a full computerised eye test, explain your prescription, and recommend lenses suited to your lifestyle. There's no obligation to buy.
Do you offer prescription sunglasses?
Absolutely. We can fit your prescription into nearly any sunglass frame in our collection, including tinted, polarised and UV400 protective lenses. Tell us your prescription (or take a free eye test in-store) and we'll have your custom sunglasses ready in a few days.
